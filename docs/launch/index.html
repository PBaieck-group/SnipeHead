What you get as the creator (Bonding Curve): when the curve reaches SHD and graduates, you automatically receive of the total supply ( tokens) plus a SHD reward. The tokens are held by the factory until then, so they can never be sold into the curve early. The remaining supply is sold on the curve and then seeded into PulseX with the collected SHD (LP burned).
SHD sent with the create tx is paired with of supply; LP is burned and the token goes live in the same transaction. Bonding Curve ignores this (set to 0).

Creation costs 10 SHD. This fee is sent to Buy & Burn and is not refundable. Instant also needs the SHD amount above approved in the same allowance.
