Mining involves smart-contract risk, and the contract may contain bugs or behave in ways you do not expect. Rewards are paid only from the reward reserve and stop if it empties. Est. APR is the current reward rate annualised, without compounding, and changes as the reserve and total mined change; it is not a promise of future returns. Nothing on this page is financial advice. Only mine what you can afford to lose.

Custom RPC

Use a PulseChain (chain ID 369) JSON-RPC endpoint over https, or http://localhost for a node on your own computer. It must allow browser (CORS) requests. It is used for all on-chain reads on this page. Your wallet's network settings are not changed and this URL is never sent to your wallet. It is saved only in this browser, and the node you choose can see your IP address and the queries this page makes.
