### 🔹 Core Components

#### 1. Token (SHD)
Standard ERC20 token that launched with built-in **launch protection**:
- Transfers were restricted until the token officially launched.
- The token was automatically launched by **Pump.Tires** (a Pump.fun clone on PulseChain) once the bonding curve filled.
- SHD has since **graduated** off the bonding curve and now trades freely on **PulseX**.
- SHD is also the token used to create and trade launches on SnipeHead Launch.

#### 2. Decentralized Mining
Fully trustless mining protocol:
- Mine SHD to earn SHD rewards
- No lockups — withdraw anytime
- Fixed reward rate
- No admin control or upgradeability
- Reward reserve is strictly separated from staked principal, with rewards hard-capped by what's actually funded — see the [Mining contract README](https://github.com/PBaieck-group/SnipeHead/blob/main/SnipeheadMiningDecentralizedV2/README.md) for the full design and 45-test Foundry suite.
- Single-file, zero-dependency dApp (v4.0.0) — see the [Mining dApp README](https://github.com/PBaieck-group/SnipeHead/blob/main/docs/mine/README.md) for how it's built and how to run your own copy.
- dApp safety features: exact-amount approvals, simulate-before-send, wallet network re-check before every transaction, receipt confirmation, and RPC failover with cross-checking.

#### 6. Deposit (Mining Reward Funding)
One-way community funding for the mining **reward reserve**. Anyone can deposit SHD into the mining contract so the system can keep paying rewards to miners. Deposits are permanent and non-refundable — depositors do not receive tokens back and do not earn mining rewards from depositing.

The dApp (v2.0) approves only the exact deposit amount (never unlimited), simulates the deposit before sending, re-checks account and chain right before each transaction, and offers a **Revoke approval** button.

#### 7. Buy & Burn
Permissionless burn tooling:
- **Direct burn** — permanently destroy SHD you already hold (via the token’s burn function).
- **Buy & Burn** — send PLS to the contract; it swaps for SHD on PulseX (TWAP-protected) and burns it. Excess PLS can remain queued for later burns.
- Helper actions: process queued PLS, refresh the price oracle, burn stuck SHD held by the contract.
- Public stats (total burned / total supply) are visible without connecting a wallet.

Burns are permanent and irreversible. PLS sent to Buy & Burn is spent, not refunded.

**Buy & Burn Contract:** `0x3677e61a7a9e79fe45a882c201365c5165a5540e`

#### 8. SnipeHead Launch
A token launch platform on PulseChain, powered by SHD. Create and trade tokens through the SnipeHead Launch factory. Every launch has a **fixed supply of 21,000,000** tokens, and you need SHD to create tokens and to trade on the Launch UI.

**Two launch modes:**
- **Instant launch** — create a token and seed a PulseX pair in one flow (create + seed + unlock), so a pool is live immediately.
- **Bonding curve** — open a curve until a graduation target is reached. Anyone can buy and sell along the curve. When the target is hit, the token can **graduate** to PulseX liquidity.

**Nominal curve parameters in the current build:**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Fixed supply | 21,000,000 |
| Graduation target | 5,000,000 SHD (nominal) |
| Creator allocation | 5% (`creatorAllocBps` = 500) |
| Creator reward | 50,000 SHD (nominal) |

**Also in the UI:** browse, search and sort launches; token pages with stats, trades and holders; a portfolio view for your holdings and recent trades; a local watchlist; and logo upload at create time.

**Fail-closed safety check:** the supply, graduation target, creator allocation/reward and virtual reserves shown on the page are checked against the live contracts at startup. If anything disagrees, **Buy / Sell / Graduate are paused** until the page matches the chain.

## 🛠️ How to Get SHD

1. **Claim from Faucet V2** ← recommended starting point for new users (1 SHD per wallet)
2. **Buy on PulseX** — SHD has graduated and trades freely, no bonding curve
3. Participate in decentralized mining

**How to get SNFT:** Mint on the [SNFT dApp](https://bafybeifczbtsz5fb3jf7kgth5ciit6ujusyuomj3efs2o5suip5ztfri6u.ipfs.inbrowser.link/) (PulseChain, public mint, max 2 per wallet) or the [SNFT-ETH dApp](https://bafybeignmap4vudo5kn5g2himozkkqmr4rnpw55s7yg3jed4veun2vsymq.ipfs.inbrowser.link/) (Ethereum, max 2 per wallet), or directly via the contracts.

**Put SHD to work:**
- Launch your own token with the [Launch dApp](https://bafybeidrowu2uerf3b47zy6t64p632jzae6qeggyvvnaik3646i3q5iqly.ipfs.inbrowser.link/) (requires SHD)

**Support the ecosystem:**
- Fund mining rewards via the [Deposit dApp](https://bafybeih4vob2upvpdzfbxyypbsbymz4hfxrxdqqomdelprbb2lx6ejsvry.ipfs.inbrowser.link/) (non-refundable)
- Permanently reduce supply via the [Buy & Burn dApp](https://bafybeigxr2quodv6ieodowhplyqvuyhoidvxipsf5nxnv434ix5gto2ila.ipfs.inbrowser.link/)
- Pin the IPFS CIDs on your own node to help keep every frontend online
